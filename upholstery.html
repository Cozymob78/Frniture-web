Complete Upholstery Solutions

Corner Sofas Reupholstery

Your corner sofa's upholstery damaged or worn? Whether it's a living room corner sofa or kitchen seating, our team of expert upholsterers specializes in professional reupholstery replacement at your home or our workshop.

  • Complete fabric replacement
  • Cushion refinishing & padding replacement
  • Structural assessment & repair

Chair Reupholstery

Reupholster and recondition dining and kitchen chairs, bringing them back to their former beauty. Professional chair reupholstery is an excellent way to give new life to your furniture pieces, whether dining chairs, office chairs, or stools.

  • Dining & office chair restoration
  • Premium fabric selection
  • Structural frame reinforcement

Bed Reupholstery

Bed reupholstery, especially headboards and bed frames, is an excellent way to completely refresh your bedroom without buying new furniture. Whether you want a style change, repair worn material, or simply refresh your space, reupholstery offers multiple benefits.

  • Headboard fabric replacement
  • Bed frame restoration
  • Comfort & style enhancement

Couch & Sofa Reupholstery

Is your extendable or fixed couch in need of reupholstery or repairs? Our experts help with fabric replacement, deteriorated mechanism repairs, cushion foam refilling, and even structural frame repairs on your sofa.

  • Complete fabric overhaul
  • Foam cushion & spring replacement
  • Mechanism & structural repairs

Armchair Reupholstery

Whether your armchair is old, worn, or simply doesn't match your home's aesthetics anymore, reupholstery can completely transform it. From classic wingback chairs to modern recliners, we restore comfort and style to any armchair.

  • Vintage & modern armchairs
  • Padding & filling restoration
  • Design fabric options

Entry Door Reupholstery

Entry door reupholstery is an efficient method to improve aesthetic appearance, thermal and acoustic insulation, and extend door lifespan without investing in a new door. This solution is popular for older apartment and house doors that need updating.

  • Thermal insulation improvement
  • Sound dampening enhancement
  • Modern aesthetic refresh

Why Choose Professional Reupholstery?

Professional furniture reupholstery provides numerous advantages over replacement. Here's why our customers choose to reupholster their valued pieces.

✓

Cost Effective

Reupholstery typically costs 50-70% less than purchasing new furniture. Give your beloved pieces a new lease of life without breaking the bank.

✓

Customization

Choose from our extensive range of premium fabrics, colors, and patterns. Create exactly the look you want for your space and lifestyle.

✓

Sustainability

Reduce waste and environmental impact by refurbishing existing furniture rather than replacing it. Support sustainable living practices.

✓

Preserves Quality

Your furniture's solid frame and structure are often excellent—only the upholstery needs attention. Preserve heirloom pieces while updating appearance.

✓

Improved Comfort

New padding, springs, and cushion filling restore comfort that worn furniture has lost. Enjoy enhanced support and durability.

✓

Professional Quality

Our experienced upholsterers ensure meticulous attention to detail, quality fabrics, and expert installation for results that rival new furniture.
