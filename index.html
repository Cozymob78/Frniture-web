What We Do

Our Repair & Restoration Services

Our master craftsmen repair, restore, and rejuvenate furniture of all styles. We carry out repairs directly in your home, or handle major restorations in our state-of-the-art workshop.

Wood & Cabinet Repairs

Complete scratch removal, dent repair, French polishing, and veneer rebuilding. We restore tables, sideboards, cabinets, and antique heirlooms back to their former luster.

  • Scratch, water mark & burn removal
  • French Polishing & color matching
  • Structural repair & joint strengthening

Leather & Vinyl Restoration

Professional cleaning, scratch fading, tear repair, and precise color-matching dye services. We restore color loss, repair pet scratches, and fix sagging padding in luxury chairs and sofas.

  • Scuff & scratch color restoration
  • Tear, burn & puncture repairs
  • Deep cleaning & protective conditioning

Reupholstery & Structural

Complete fabric replacement, foam cushion refilling, spring replacements, and frame repairs. Make your saggy or outdated sofa look and feel brand new with premium upholstery.

  • Cushion foam & spring replacements
  • Webbing & frame structural repair
  • Wide range of designer fabrics & velvet
