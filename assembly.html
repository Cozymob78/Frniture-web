Assembly Services

Furniture Assembly Services We Offer

From home kitchens to office workspaces, our team builds and installs furniture safely and accurately. We work with ready-made fittings, flat-pack systems, and bespoke units.

Kitchen Furniture Assembly

We assemble kitchen cabinets, worktops, wall units and integrated appliances with precision. No matter the brand or design, we can install your kitchen safely and professionally.

  • Cabinet alignment and fixing
  • Worktop installation support
  • Appliance enclosure assembly

Wardrobe & Closet Assembly

From sliding door wardrobes to built-in storage, we assemble and install wardrobes, dressers and wardrobe systems. We ensure doors move smoothly and structures remain secure.

  • Sliding door and hinged wardrobe assembly
  • Built-in storage installation
  • Secure fixing and adjustment

Table & Chair Assembly

We build dining tables, coffee tables, desks and chair sets quickly and cleanly. Whether flat-packed or boxed, we assemble furniture with care so it is stable, aligned and ready to use.

  • Dining and living room furniture
  • Office desk and chair assembly
  • Secure fasteners and leveling

Office Furniture Assembly

Professional installation of office desks, shelving, cupboards and workspace systems. We assemble modern and ergonomic office furniture with precision and minimal disruption.

  • Desks, shelving and storage units
  • Space planning and installation
  • Corporate and home offices

Sliding Door Furniture

We assemble wardrobes and storage systems with sliding doors, including mirrored and glass panels. Our installers ensure rails, wheels and guides work smoothly every time.

  • Sliding door wardrobe systems
  • Mirror and glass panel installation
  • Track adjustment and leveling

Upholstered Furniture Assembly

We install upholstered beds, sofas, armchairs and corner seating. Your upholstered furniture is assembled with care so cushions, frames and mechanisms are aligned and secure.

  • Upholstered beds and sofa frames
  • Cushion and mechanism setup
  • Stable and comfortable results
